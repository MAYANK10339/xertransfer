About XerTransfer

XerTransfer is a modern peer-to-peer file transfer platform with liquid glass aesthetics and instant zero-latency stream negotiation.

Zero-Delay Instant Connection

Pre-gathered ICE mesh and bundled SDP exchange connect sender and receiver in milliseconds without waiting.

Folder Structure Preservation

Upload entire directory trees and save as structured ZIP or native real folders directly to disk.

100% Zero Corruption

Every chunk carries an 8-byte framing header ensuring sequenced reassembly and byte-for-byte fidelity.

Multi-Theme & Liquid Glass

Switch between 4 curated themes and toggle dynamic liquid glass refraction with specular lighting.

Cross Platform

Works flawlessly across Android, iOS (iPhone/iPad), Windows, Mac, and Linux inside any browser.

100% Free & Private

End-to-end DTLS encrypted. Data flows directly between devices without touching any third-party storage.
