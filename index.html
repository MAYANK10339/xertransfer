Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about XerTransfer and its zero-cloud P2P engine.

XerTransfer utilizes WebRTC DataChannels to stream data directly from your device's memory/disk to the receiver's device in real-time. Because your files never pass through or get stored on any intermediary cloud server, there are no artificial file size limits.

To achieve maximum hardware transfer speed, connect both devices to the same Wi-Fi network or turn on a Mobile Hotspot on one phone and connect your PC or second phone to it. Transfer will happen directly across the local LAN without consuming your mobile internet data!
